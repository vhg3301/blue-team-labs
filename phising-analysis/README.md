# Phishing Email Analysis Lab

A Blue Team project focused on phishing email investigation, social engineering analysis, and threat assessment.

This repository contains the analysis of a phishing email impersonating Netflix and a security operations team in an attempt to trick users into clicking a malicious account recovery link.

---

## Objectives

* Identify phishing indicators
* Analyze social engineering techniques
* Document Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)
* Assess email-based threats
* Practice Blue Team investigation workflows
* Improve phishing detection skills

## Case Study

### Netflix Account Recovery Phishing Email

**Scenario:**

An email impersonating Netflix claimed that unauthorized access had been detected on the victim's account and urged immediate action through a recovery link.

**Analysis Covered:**

* Sender verification
* Social engineering techniques
* Credential theft indicators
* Domain legitimacy assessment
* Risk evaluation
* Defensive recommendations

## Key Takeaways

* Modern phishing emails can be highly convincing.
* Domain verification is often more important than email appearance.
* Urgency and fear remain some of the most effective social engineering techniques.
* Users should verify requests through official channels before taking action.
